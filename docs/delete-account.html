Delete your PrepWisely Team account

PrepWisely Team

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PrepWisely Team and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar in the top corner.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email dharanigowthamsampath@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, using the subject "Delete my PrepWisely Team account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and password.
  • Your membership in every team.
  • Your personal to-dos in every team.
  • Any team where you are the only member, along with all of its tasks.

What stays with your teams

  • Shared (common) tasks you created stay in the team for its other members, but are no longer linked to you.
  • Tasks that were assigned to you become unassigned.
  • If you owned a team that still has members, ownership passes to the member who joined earliest.

Copies in backups and server logs expire on their normal schedule, normally within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for more.
