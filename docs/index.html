The idea

To-do apps grew into big tools. Small teams didn't.

Most task apps are now built for big companies, with boards, sprints, workflows, custom fields and integrations. A team of four or five doesn't need any of that. You just need to say "Priya, please send the invoice by Friday", not have it get lost in WhatsApp, and know when it's done. That's all this app does.

Big project tools

  • Boards, sprints, workflows and custom fields

  • Training the team to use it, and then nobody does

A look inside

What's inside

Just the essentials.

Four things, done well. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use this.

Remind a colleague

Write what needs doing, pick who should do it, and they have it. Or leave it open for whoever's free.

By when

Add a date and mark it urgent if it is. Things due today and overdue stand out on everyone's list.

See when it's done

When your colleague ticks it off, you see it right away. No chasing, no "did you do that?" messages.

Your own reminders

Private to-dos just for you, in the same list as what your team asked you to do.

What we left out, on purpose

Kanban boards Sprints Gantt charts Custom fields Workflows Time tracking Integrations Admin settings

If your team needs these, a big tool is the right choice. If you just need reminders that don't get lost, this is it.

Install on Android

  1. Download the app on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser: allow it.
  3. If Play Protect warns that it doesn't know the app, choose Install anyway. It warns because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.
